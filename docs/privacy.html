Privacy Policy

AI Test Data Filler · Last updated: 2 October 2026 · Contact: andris.priede@gmail.com

AI Test Data Filler fills web forms with fictional test data written by an AI service that you choose and pay for yourself.

What the extension sends, and when

Only when you click Fill this page, the extension sends the following to the AI provider you selected (Anthropic for Claude, or OpenAI for ChatGPT), using your own API key:

  • a description of the fillable form fields on the current page: labels, field names, input types, placeholders, allowed options, and length and format limits;
  • the option currently selected in drop-downs and radio buttons on the page (for example a chosen country), so the generated data can match it;
  • the language and instructions you typed into the extension.

It does not send values you have typed into the page, passwords, cookies, browsing history or other page text. Password, payment card and security-code fields are ignored.

What is stored

Your API key(s), model names and preferences are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced, are never sent to the developer, and are sent only to the provider they belong to.

What we do not do

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no sale or sharing of data, and no remote code. The developer receives no data from your use of the extension.

Third parties

Requests to Anthropic or OpenAI are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Their charges for API use are billed to your account.

Your control

Delete your keys on the extension's setup page, or uninstall the extension, to remove all stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
